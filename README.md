### ALGO-000069: Strong bounded-depth Frege bounds for random 3-CNF
- **Subfield:** Proof complexity and metacomplexity
- **Open:** Prove superpolynomial, ideally exponential, bounded-depth Frege refutation-size lower bounds for random unsatisfiable 3-CNF formulas with a sufficiently large constant clause-to-variable ratio.
- **Status:** A 2024 result obtains \(n^{1+\varepsilon_d}\)-type bounds for fixed proof depth \(d\). The stronger growth targets remain unresolved.
- **Type:** impossibility
- **Why:** Would connect proof complexity to experimentally hard random satisfiability instances.
- **Key works:** Gryaznov and Talebanfard (2024), *Bounded-Depth Frege Lower Bounds for Random 3-CNFs via Deterministic Restrictions*, arXiv:2403.02275.
- **Related:** Statistical physics—random-SAT phase transitions and algorithmic barriers.
